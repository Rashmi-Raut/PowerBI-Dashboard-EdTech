# Course Enrollment & Revenue Analysis – Power BI

## Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides an interactive analysis of course enrollments, revenue, discounts, payment collections, course performance, and customer characteristics for an EdTech business.

The dashboard helps stakeholders understand enrollment trends, revenue contribution, collection performance, and customer segments to support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall enrollment performance
- Identify high-performing courses
- Analyze revenue trends
- Identify areas of improvement
- Support data-driven business decisions

## Key KPIs

Total Enrollments - 1,000 
Total Courses     - 25 
Course Fee        - 17M
Discount          - 2M
Actual Course Fee - 15M 
Amount Paid       - 13M
Collection Rate   - 87.13%|

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Excel

## Dashboard Features

- Interactive KPI cards
- Course-level performance analysis
- Country-wise revenue and enrollment analysis
- Payment type analysis
- Collection rate by employment level
- Revenue analysis by qualification and employment level
- Dynamic slicers for:
  - Course Category
  - Course Name
  - Country
  - Qualification
  - Employment Level
  - Discount Applied

## Key Insights

1."India" is the strongest market, contributing approximately 4.9M revenue, considerably higher than the other countries shown.

2."Financial Modeling & Valuation" has the highest number of enrollments in the course table at 50, while also generating approximately 844K revenue.

3."Artificial Intelligence for Business" appears among the strongest course categories in terms of actual course fee, at approximately 1.78M.

4.The overall collection rate is 87.13%, indicating that most of the actual course fee has been collected, while there is still a collection gap.

5."Mid-Level employees" have the highest collection rate (88.22%), while Managers have the lowest (83.69%) among the employment levels displayed.

6."Online payments" dominate enrollments across the countries shown, with "India" having a particularly large share of online enrollments.

7.The dashboard shows that discounting has a meaningful impact on realized revenue, with approximately 2M in total discounts against the 17M course fee.
